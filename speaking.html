SPEAKING

I use my voice to start conversations.

From classrooms to panels and community spaces, I speak about the things I am learning, the questions young people are asking and the possibilities I believe we should explore.

MY APPROACH

I don't want people to simply hear me.

I want them to leave thinking about something differently.

I enjoy creating spaces where young people can ask questions, challenge ideas, share experiences and discover that their voices matter.

My speaking experiences include panels, school outreach, moderation, mentorship conversations, youth leadership spaces and community sessions.

02 SPEAKING EXPERIENCES
2026

Girls in Bloom Ambassador Training

Invited to speak to a new cohort of Girls in Bloom ambassadors about my experience as a 2025 Global Ambassador. I shared lessons from taking the experience beyond participation and putting my Bloom With Dignity project into action, including what I learned from planning, coordinating and implementing the project.

2025–26

School Outreach & Mentorship

Participated in school outreaches and mentorship sessions with young people, creating spaces for students to ask questions, learn, reflect and engage in conversations around issues that affect their everyday lives.

2025

Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador

Selected as a Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador in 2025. The experience gave me the opportunity to develop my leadership and advocacy skills and, most importantly, turn what I was learning into action through Bloom With Dignity. Bloom With Dignity brought together ambassadors from Nigeria, Kenya, Mozambique and Zimbabwe to work on menstrual health and hygiene, access to menstrual products and HPV vaccine awareness for girls.

2024

Girls in Bloom Global Summit

Co-moderated a Girls X Technology conversation with Ms. Tosin Durotoye, founder of The Bloom Africa, bringing together conversations around girls, technology and possibilities.

2023

Slum2School Global Volunteer Summit

Participated as a youth panelist, sharing perspectives from the student experience of education in Nigeria and reflecting on my journey as a Slum2School learner.
