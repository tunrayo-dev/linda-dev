Meet Motunrayo.

Youth Leader · Technologist · Speaker · Girls' Empowerment Advocate

WHO I AM

Building a life around impact.

Motunrayo Linda Idowu is a Nigerian youth leader, emerging technologist, speaker and girls' empowerment advocate passionate about creating opportunities for young people to learn, lead and transform their communities.

Her work sits at the intersection of technology, youth leadership, girls' empowerment, education and social impact.

She founded Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle, a supportive community where girls can connect, learn, express themselves and grow. She is also a volunteer with Book Bridge Foundation and YAWAI Initiative.

Motunrayo was one of only three Nigerian teenagers selected for PAYLP, a leadership and cultural exchange programme in the United States of America.

VISION

Technology, voice and opportunity.

Motunrayo's long-term vision is to become a leading technologist, global public speaker and social-impact leader, using technology and her platform to create meaningful opportunities for young people across Africa.
