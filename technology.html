TECHNOLOGY

From wondering how things work to learning how to build.

Technology started as something I was curious about. It has gradually become one of the ways I imagine creating change.

THE BEGINNING

I originally wanted to be an architect.

I loved the idea of building things.

I wondered how people could imagine a structure in their heads and then turn that idea into something real.

Technology eventually gave me another way to think about building.

Through STEM and coding programmes, I began exploring programming, web development and digital tools. My curiosity gradually moved toward technology and eventually toward data.

WHY TECHNOLOGY?

Because I don't just want to understand the world. I want to build things for it.

I am particularly interested in data and computer-related fields, and in using technology to address real problems faced by young people and communities.
