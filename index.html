01 / ABOUT

Building a life at the intersection of technology, leadership & impact.

Motunrayo Linda Idowu is passionate about creating spaces where young people—especially girls—can build confidence, develop leadership skills and access opportunities.

Her journey spans STEM, technology, youth leadership, public speaking, girls' empowerment and social impact. She was one of only three Nigerian teenagers selected for the Pan African Youth Leadership Program (PAYLP), a leadership and cultural exchange programme in the United States of America.

She is a Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador, a volunteer with Book Bridge Foundation and YAWAI Initiative, and the founder of Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle.

02 / IMPACT

Turning opportunities into action.

Her work with girls and young people combines education, advocacy, community and leadership.

01

GiBGAP Community Project

As a Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador, Motunrayo worked with teammates across Nigeria, Kenya, Zimbabwe and Mozambique to equip girls with approximately one year's worth of menstrual products.

The project also provided menstrual health and hygiene education and raised awareness about HPV vaccination.
