PUBLIC IDENTITY

Motunrayo Linda Idowu
also known as Motunrayo Idowu.

Motunrayo Linda Idowu is a Nigerian youth leader, technologist, speaker and girls' empowerment advocate. Public references to her work may use the shorter name Motunrayo Idowu.

This page brings those public-facing experiences together in one place so visitors can follow her work across youth leadership, technology, girls' empowerment and social impact.

SELECTED APPEARANCES

SUMMIT · 2024

Girls in Bloom Summit

Co-moderated the GirlsXTechnology session alongside Tosin Durotoye, the founder of The Bloom Africa contributing to a conversation connecting girls, technology and opportunity.

SUMMIT

Slum2School Africa Global Volunteer Summit

Participated as a panelist, contributing her perspective as a young leader and member of the Slum2School community.

LEADERSHIP

Pan African Youth Leadership Program

Selected as one of only three Nigerian teenagers for PAYLP, a leadership and cultural exchange programme in the United States of America.

COMMUNITY IMPACT

Girls in Bloom Global Ambassadors Program

As a Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador, Motunrayo participated in the GiBGAP 2025 social-impact project with teammates across Nigeria, Kenya, Zimbabwe and Mozambique.

The project equipped girls with approximately one year's worth of menstrual products and provided education on menstrual health and hygiene, alongside awareness about HPV vaccination.
