Motunrayo Linda Idowu

Youth Leader • Technologist • Speaker • Girls' Empowerment Advocate

Lagos, Nigeria

I am Motunrayo Linda Idowu, a Nigerian youth leader, emerging technologist, speaker and girls' empowerment advocate interested in the intersection of technology, data, education, leadership and social impact.

My journey began in Makoko, Lagos, where access to education shaped how I think about opportunity and possibility. Through education, STEM programmes, leadership experiences, community work and public speaking, I have continued learning, building and using what I learn to create opportunities for others.

What I do

- Technology & Data — exploring programming, web development, SQL, Microsoft Power BI, data science and digital tools.
- Public Speaking — speaking, moderating and facilitating conversations around education, technology, girls' empowerment, youth leadership and personal growth.
- Girls' Empowerment — creating spaces and initiatives that help girls connect, learn, build confidence and grow.
- Youth Leadership — turning opportunities into action through community projects, mentorship, advocacy and service.

Community & impact

Girls Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle

A girl-led online community I founded to bring girls from different backgrounds and countries together to connect, learn, reflect, laugh and grow.

Bloom With Dignity

A Girls in Bloom initiative focused on menstrual health and hygiene, access to menstrual products and HPV vaccine awareness. The project reached approximately 70+ girls through a team of young women from Nigeria, Kenya, Mozambique and Zimbabwe.

LUMINDA

A technology-based student opportunity concept inspired by the challenge of finding scholarships, programmes, fellowships, internships, volunteering opportunities and other opportunities. LUMINDA is currently in development and has not launched yet.
