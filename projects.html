GIBGAP

Girls in Bloom Global Ambassadors Project

As a Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador, Motunrayo worked with teammates across Nigeria, Kenya, Zimbabwe and Mozambique.

The project equipped girls with approximately one year's worth of menstrual products, provided menstrual health and hygiene education, and raised awareness about HPV vaccination.

GIRLS' UNPLUGGED

Bloom & Glow Circle

Motunrayo founded Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle to create a supportive space where girls can connect, learn, express themselves, grow and develop confidence and leadership.
